# Spirit Ward — Table Reference Card

*Print one per hospital, plus one for Control. Spirit Ward v0.1 · Pattern Language v0.1*

---

## The marks

| Mark | Name | Applied by | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| ◉ Sticker (face) | *possessed* | Control | Unplayable until cured. Counts on the outbreak track. |
| ⌾ Punch | *drained* | you — announce first | Permanent. Half rank in ward work, **double rank in banishments**. |
| ✎ Ink dot (face) | *cure-dot* | Control, at cure | This card has been possessed before. |
| ☗ **Ink on BACK** | **ghost sigil** | **Control only** | Permanently haunted. Identifiable face-down by everyone, forever. Travels with the card. |

- **Second possession of a cure-dotted card → Control sigils its back.**
- **3 marks = Vessel**: name it. Only usable in banishments (double rank) or as the card banished.
- Routine marks: announce, then mark. Sigils and dots: Control's marker only.

## Card values

Number = rank · J Q K = 11 · Ace = 1 · Drained = half (round up), **double in banishments**.

## Turn sequence (×6)

1. **Outbreak** — Control runs the script line.
2. **Conference (4 min)** — open across hospitals: mandated surge transfer first, then free trades.
3. **Ward Phase** — your hospital only. **2 actions each.**
4. **Accounting** — track +1 per possessed card citywide, read aloud.

## Ward Phase actions

| Action | What happens | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **Treat** | Play ♥ ≥ patient's rank → seal off, Control dots the patient, your ♥ is punched and returned. | return, marked |
| **Ward** | Play any ♣, name a rank → that rank is immune in your hospital this turn. ♣ punched, returned. | return, marked |
| **Banish** | Tear a sigiled card or Vessel + commit rank **12+ across 2+ suits** (punched, returned; drained ♥ double). **Track −2, Standing +3.** Stand up; Control witnesses. | spend |
| **Pass** | — | — |

## Transfers

- **Mandated:** most-possessed hospital sends one possessed card to least-possessed. Refusal: Administrator spends ♦ totalling ≥ the card's rank (into the box).
- **Voluntary:** anything, during Conference. Marks, dots, sigils and back colours travel with the card.
- **The ghost jumps:** a sigiled card arriving in your hospital → immediately seal one card of equal rank, if you have one.

## The outbreak track — public

Starts 0 · +1 per possessed card at each Accounting · −2 per banishment.
**City falls at 15 (two hospitals) / 20 (three). If it falls, nobody wins.**

## Control's script (summary)

T1: 1×rank 4 each · T2: 1×4, 1×8 · T3: 1×7, 1×face + **Poltergeist joker** (sealed 10, banish-only, to one hospital) · T4: 3× ranks 3/6/9 · T5: 2×any, 1×Ace + **Medium joker** (wild 10 for banishments, to fewest-possessed) · T6: 2×any.
Named rank all sealed/warded/absent → step up one rank.

## Scoring (only if the city stands)

+1 per cure · +3 per banishment · +3 per met secret goal · −2 to the most-possessed hospital at the end. Standing columns hidden until the end. Highest hospital wins.
