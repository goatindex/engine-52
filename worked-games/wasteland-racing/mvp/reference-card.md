# Scrap Circuit — Table Reference Card

*Print one per crew. Scrap Circuit v0.1 · Pattern Language v0.1*

---

## The marks — announce, then mark · faces only, never backs

| Mark | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ⌾ **Punch** | *damage* | Card is **Worn**: full rank in races, **+1 Fame each in a winning packet**; half rank (round up) in pit work. |
| ◉ **Sticker** | *breakdown* | Unplayable until a Mechanic repairs it. |
| ✎ **Ink** | *sponsor tag* | From the word list ONLY: **APEX OIL · IRONWORKS · CHOPSHOP · REDLINE · VULTURE GIN · MOTORMAW.** Winning packet: +2 Fame. Spent to Vault: −1 Fame. One per card. |

**3 marks = Legend:** name it. Can't race, can't be spent. **+5 Fame if held at the end.** Rival promoters may buy it.

## Card values

Number = rank · J Q K = 11 · Ace = 1 · Joker = 10 any suit, spent after one race · **Worn = full rank in races, half in the pit.**

## Roles and swap bands

| Suit | Role | Collects | Special |
|---|---|---|---|
| ♠ | Driver | Face cards | — |
| ♣ | Mechanic | 2–5 | Repairs |
| ♥ | Gunner | 6–10 | Beats hazards |
| ♦ | Promoter | Aces | Sponsors, Hype, deals |

## Race sequence (×6)

1. **Entry fee** — every crew: 1 card into the Vault. Always.
2. **Commit** — face-down packet, up to the race's limit, from any hands. Reveal together.
3. **Hazard** — no ♥ ≥ hazard in your packet? Highest committed card takes a **punch**, lowest takes a **breakdown**.
4. **Speed** — total ranks. Highest wins purse; second takes minor. Tie: highest single card.
5. **League collects** — committed **♠ ♣ → Vault forever.** Committed **♥ ♦ → punched, returned.** Winner: +1 Fame per Worn, +2 per sponsored card in the packet.

## Pit phase — 2 actions each, between races

| Action | What happens | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **Repair** | Play ♣ (punched, returns) → clear one breakdown. | return, marked |
| **Promote** | Ink one word-list sponsor onto a crew card. | mark |
| **Hype** | Spend a ♦ to the Vault → **+1 Fame now.** | spend |
| **Deal** | Trade cross-crew; tear a card to seal a pact (rank = stake; breaker pays it at the end). | travel / spend |
| **Pass** | — | — |

## Jokers — the comeback

Win a race immediately after losing one → take a joker (max 2). Rank 10, any suit, one race, then Vault.

## The floor

Fewer total cards than every rival crew → **+3 speed and no entry fee.** No playable cards? You can still Promote and Deal.

## The Audit — game end

**Every full 3 cards in the Vault: −1 Fame to EVERY crew.** Then +4 per met secret goal, +5 per held Legend, ± pact stakes.
**Most Fame wins. The Fame track is public all game. There is no gate — only the bill.**
