# Scrap Circuit — Minimum Prototype

*v0.1 — the spend-heavy, ungated prototype for the Engine 52 pattern language. Two to four race crews, six races. Built against Pattern Language v0.1.*

---

## What this document is

The third evidence-gathering instrument, and the engine's deliberate stress test. Where *Salvage Run* gated its shared goal and hid its scores, **Scrap Circuit knowingly departs from two starred patterns** — the shared tier is a weighted commons with **no gate** (testing Pattern 5's claim that ungated semi-coop collapses into theatre), and the Fame track is **fully public** (testing Pattern 6's bash-the-leader prediction; racing leagues have standings, so the genre demands the violation). It also runs the lifecycle the other way: **spend-heavy with SHRINKING HANDS as the designed arc** (Patterns 11, 14), scars that *help* in the marquee system (20, inverted from Salvage Run), the boxed word-list resolution of ink (17), and face-down anonymity under real load — including the known leak that **punched cards are identifiable face-down by feel** (a live finding for Pattern 19, Resolution A).

**Register:** boxed tabletop, convention pace (Pattern 1). **Players:** 6–16, as two to four crews of 3–4. **Time:** ~75 minutes.

## The story, in one paragraph

After the collapse, the League put the survivors back to work — racing. The rich watch from the towers; the crews bleed engine parts and entry fees into the League's vault and get paid in Fame. Fans love a battle-scarred car and a comeback story. Every race makes the League richer and your hand thinner. Win anyway.

## Components (Pattern 25)

Per crew: 1 standard deck (**different back colour per crew**), 1 hole punch, stickers, 1 fine marker.
Shared: this document with the Race Schedule and the **Sponsor Word List**, a **public Fame track** (paper), a space in the table's centre marked **THE VAULT**.
*(Accessibility, Pattern 24: square stickers replace punches — but note the face-down feel test below is then void; record which variant you played.)*

## Setup

1. Each crew takes a deck and sets its two jokers aside, face up — they are earned, not dealt (Pattern 23, boxed resolution).
2. **Suits as roles (Pattern 7):**

   | Suit | Role | Does |
   |---|---|---|
   | ♠ | **Driver** | nerve — speed is spent through them |
   | ♣ | **Mechanic** | the car — parts, repairs |
   | ♥ | **Gunner** | covering fire on the hazards |
   | ♦ | **Promoter** | fans, sponsors, the League |

   *Three-player crew:* the Driver also takes ♥ (Driver-Gunner). All crews use the same arrangement.
3. **Turn-zero swap (Pattern 8):** standard bands — faces to ♠, 2–5 to ♣, 6–10 to ♥, Aces to ♦. Each player gives each teammate one card from that teammate's band.
4. Each player secretly picks one individual goal (see Goals).
5. The Fame track goes where everyone can see it. It stays public all game (Pattern 6 — deliberate departure, see watch-list).

## Goals (Patterns 4, 5, 6)

- **Shared (weighted, NOT gated):** the Vault is a commons. At game end, **every full 3 cards in the Vault cost every crew 1 Fame** (the League Audit). No gate: the game cannot be "lost" collectively — only bled. *This is the test.*
- **Crew:** most Fame after the Audit wins. **The Fame track is public throughout.**
- **Individual (secret, worth 4 Fame):**

  | Role | Choose one |
  |---|---|
  | Driver | (D1) Personally commit the highest card in the winning packet of the Championship. (D2) Win two consecutive races. |
  | Mechanic | (M1) End with 4+ punched cards across the crew — the car held together. (M2) Clear 3+ breakdowns. |
  | Gunner | (G1) Take no hazard damage in three consecutive races. (G2) Personally commit the ♥ that beats the hazard in the two highest-purse races. |
  | Promoter | (P1) Have 3+ sponsor-tagged cards race in winning packets. (P2) End with a poached card (another crew's back colour) in your crew. |

## The card lifecycle sentence (Pattern 9)

> **In this game, racing spends speed to the League forever; repairs and stunts return the card punched; sponsorships and poaching send cards to other crews.**

Spent cards go **face up into the Vault** — the League's extraction is public and grows all game (Pattern 5's receipts, writ large). Unmarked cards are coins; marked cards are objects (Pattern 13).

## The mark grammar (Patterns 16, 17)

| Mark | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Punch** | *damage* | Permanent: the card is **Worn**. **Full rank in races, +1 Fame each when in a winning packet** (fans love wreckage — Pattern 20, inverted: scars shine in the marquee system). Half rank (round up) in pit work. |
| **Sticker** | *breakdown* | Status: unplayable until a Mechanic repairs it. |
| **Ink** | *sponsor tag* | **From the closed word list only** (Pattern 17, boxed resolution): APEX OIL · IRONWORKS · CHOPSHOP · REDLINE · VULTURE GIN · MOTORMAW. One tag per card, counts as a mark. A sponsored card in a winning packet: **+2 Fame.** A sponsored card spent to the Vault: **−1 Fame** (the sponsor is furious). |

Announce, then mark (Pattern 18). **Faces only — backs are never marked** (Pattern 19, Resolution A): race commits are face-down and must stay anonymous. *Known leak under test: a punched card is identifiable as punched, face-down, by feel and sight of the hole.* **Terminal form (Pattern 21):** the third mark makes the card a **Legend** — name it aloud, tag the name. A Legend cannot be committed or spent; it sits face-up before its crew and is worth **5 Fame at the end if the crew still holds it** — and rival promoters may buy it (travel) at any negotiated price.

## The three systems

### System 1 — The Race (verbs: spend to the Vault; return punched)

Each turn is one race from the schedule. In order:

1. **Entry fee:** every crew puts one card (any) into the Vault. Always. The League takes first (Pattern 5's commons pressure).
2. **Commit:** each crew secretly assembles a face-down packet — up to the race's card limit, from any members' hands. Reveal simultaneously.
3. **Hazard check:** each crew's packet must contain a ♥ of rank ≥ the race's hazard. If not: the packet's highest card takes a **punch** and its lowest takes a **breakdown sticker** before scoring.
4. **Speed:** total the packet's ranks (faces 11, Aces 1; Worn cards full rank). Highest speed wins the purse; second takes the minor purse. Ties: the packet containing the single highest card wins.
5. **The League collects:** all committed **♠ and ♣ go to the Vault** (spent forever). Committed **♥ and ♦ are punched and returned.** Bonus Fame: +1 per Worn card and +2 per sponsored card in the *winning* packet.

### System 2 — The Pit (verb: return, punched) — between races, 2 actions per player

- **Repair:** play any ♣ (punched, returned) → clear one breakdown in your crew.
- **Promote:** ink one sponsor tag from the word list onto any crew card.
- **Hype:** spend any ♦ into the Vault → **+1 Fame now.** (Selling out: it feeds the Audit. Watch who does it.)
- **Deal:** trade cards cross-crew — sponsorship poaching, Legend sales, pacts. Marks and back colours travel (Pattern 12). To seal a binding pact, tear a card between crews (Pattern 22): rank = stake; the wronged crew reveals its half at the end for the stake in Fame from the breaker.
- **Pass.**

### System 3 — The League (the commons)

The Vault grows by entry fees, spent speed, and Hype. At game end, the **Audit**: every full 3 cards in the Vault → **−1 Fame to every crew.** The League cannot lose. The only collective lever is restraint — smaller packets, fewer hypes — and restraint costs races. *(Pattern 5 predicts this cooperation will be theatre. Find out.)*

### The jokers (Pattern 23)

A crew that **wins a race immediately after losing one** takes a joker into any member's hand — the comeback story, and the fans roar. A joker is rank 10, any suit, committable to one race, then spent to the Vault. Two per crew, maximum.

### The floor (Pattern 14)

Hands shrink by design — that's the arc. The protected floor: a crew holding **fewer total cards than every rival crew** adds **+3 speed** to its packets ("nothing left to lose") and pays **no entry fee.** A player with no playable cards still acts in the Pit (Promote and Deal need no cards in hand to give).

## Race Schedule (v0.1 numbers — tune in playtest)

| Race | Name | Card limit | Hazard (♥ ≥) | Purse / minor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Junkyard Sprint | 3 | 5 | 5 / 2 |
| 2 | Canyon Run | 3, must include a ♣ | 7 | 6 / 3 |
| 3 | Ambush Alley | 3 | 8, **♥ count double speed** | 7 / 3 |
| 4 | The Gauntlet | 4 | 10 | 8 / 4 |
| 5 | Deadman's Loop | 4 | J (11), **Worn cards +2 Fame each (winning packet)** | 9 / 4 |
| 6 | The Championship | 4, minimum 3 | Q (11) | 12 / 6 |

## End and scoring

After race 6: the **Audit** (−1 Fame per full 3 Vault cards, every crew), then +4 per met individual goal, +5 per held Legend, ± pact stakes. **Most Fame wins.** There is no gate; there is only the bill.

## Budget arithmetic (Pattern 9 check)

13 cards; entry fees cost 6 per crew; racing spends ~2 ♠/♣ per race per crew (~10–14); repairs and hazards punch the rest. Expected end-state: hands of **4–7 cards per player, heavily marked** — the designed shrinkage arc (Pattern 14), with the floor rule as the parachute. If crews are benched before race 5, cut entry fees to alternate races before touching packet limits.

## What this prototype tests

| Hypothesis under test | Pattern | What to watch |
|---|---|---|
| **Ungated semi-coop collapses into theatre** — Pattern 5's core claim, tested by violation | 5 | Did anyone exercise restraint for the commons? Did Vault talk happen at all after race 2? |
| **Public standing invites bash-the-leader** — Pattern 6 tested by violation | 6 | Pacts against the Fame leader; kingmaking in race 6 |
| Spend-heavy shrinkage is an arc, not elimination | 11, 14 | Was the floor rule reached? Was a small hand *tense* or *boring*? |
| Scars-as-powers in the marquee system makes players court damage | 20 | Did anyone skip a Gunner card *to get* punched before race 5? |
| The closed word-list keeps ink adjudicable in the boxed register | 17 | Sponsor disputes; anyone wanting to write freely |
| Punches leak through face-down anonymity | 19-A | Did players read opponents' packets by holes before reveal? Record instances — this finding feeds Pattern 19 regardless of resolution |
| Terminal form as a held trophy (vs Salvage Run's crisis fuel) | 21 | Were Legends kept, sold, or regretted? |
| Travel as poaching keeps role identity intact at medium volume | 12 vs 7 | Did hands still read as roles by race 6? |
| Torn pacts function in a fully adversarial setting | 22 | Pacts made? Honoured? Was breaking one ever worth it? |
| The comeback joker manufactures drama on schedule | 23 | Did a joker decide a race? Table reaction |
