# Salvage Run — Table Reference Card

*Print one per crew. Salvage Run v0.1 · Pattern Language v0.1*

---

## The marks — announce, then mark, in view of the table

| Mark | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ⌾ **Punch** | *stress* | Permanent: card is **Worn**. Half rank (round up) at sites. Sells +2 each. Full rank at Deep Sites. |
| ◉ **Sticker** | *seal* | Status: card is jammed. Unplayable until a Medic clears it. |
| ✎ **Ink** | *tag* | Information only. Words tell, never do. |

- **Faces only. Never mark a card's back.**
- **3 marks max.** The third mark makes a **Relic**: name it, tag the name. Relics can't work sites or be sold — they count **double rank at the reactor**.
- An unannounced mark is void.

## Card values

Number cards = rank · **J Q K = 11** · **Ace = 1** · Joker = 10, any suit, spent after use.
**Worn = half rank at sites (round up), full rank at Deep Sites.**

## Roles and swap bands

| Suit | Role | Collects (turn-zero swap) | Special |
|---|---|---|---|
| ♠ | Breaker | Face cards (J Q K) | — |
| ♣ | Wrench | Ranks 2–5 | — |
| ♥ | Medic | Ranks 6–10 | **Mend**: play any ♥ (it takes a punch) to clear one seal in your crew |
| ♦ | Broker | Aces | **Only voice across crews**; tears contract cards |

## Turn sequence (×6)

1. **Expose** — read this turn's two sites.
2. **Council (3 min)** — say what *you* can do; never tell another player what to do. Brokers may cross the table.
3. **Actions** — alternating crews, **2 actions each**: **Work** · **Sell** · **Mend** · **Deal** · **Pass**.
4. **Crisis** — end of turns **3** and **6** only.
5. **Cleanup** — punched cards return to hands; start crew passes over.

## The actions

| Action | What happens | Verb |
|---|---|---|
| **Work** | Crew commits cards meeting a site's appetite. Score SP. All committed cards **punched and returned**; tag one with the site name. **Mishap: lowest committed card takes a seal.** | return, marked |
| **Sell** | Box one card forever. Score its rank, **+2 per punch**. No Relics. | spend |
| **Mend** | Medic only — clear one seal (the played ♥ takes a punch). | return, marked |
| **Deal** | Execute a trade or reunite contract halves. Traded cards keep marks and back colour. | travel |
| **Pass** | — | — |

## The reactor — both crews together, open contributions, cards spent (torn and boxed)

| | Threshold | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| **Tremor** (end T3) | rank **24+** | 1 ♥ and 1 ♣ from *each* crew |
| **Final Collapse** (end T6) | rank **36+** (42+ if Tremor failed) | 2 suits from *each* crew |

Tremor failed → every player seals their highest unmarked card. **Final Collapse failed → reactor breaches: nobody wins.** Relics count double here.

## Contracts (Broker)

Tear one of your cards at the handshake; each crew holds half. Rank = stake. Reunite to execute. Broken deal: wronged crew reveals its half at game end — breaker **loses** the stake in SP, wronged crew **gains** it.

## Jokers

First site your crew claims using cards from **3+ different players** → take one of your jokers into any hand. Max two.

## Scoring (only if the reactor survives)

Sites + sales + contract awards/penalties + **5 SP per met secret goal**. Keep your SP column **hidden** until the end. Highest crew total wins; most personally-generated SP is MVP.
